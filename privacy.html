Privacy Policy

Effective October 29, 2026

This policy describes how Oasis Technologies LLC, doing business as Gamers Oasis ("we", "us"), handles information in our mobile games, including Wingfold (the "App").

Information we do not collect

We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location. Your game progress is stored on your device.

Third-party services

The App uses the following Google services, each of which may process data under its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob (optional rewarded ads only). AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, and ad interaction data to show and measure ads. Ads are never shown unless you choose to watch one. Where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK, and some US states), we ask for your consent using Google's User Messaging Platform, and you can change your choice in the App's settings. If you buy the Sanctuary Pass, the ads SDK is not loaded.
  • Google Play Games Services (optional sign-in, cloud save, and achievements). If you sign in, your Play Games profile identifier and saved game data are stored by Google so your progress can be restored.
  • Google Play Billing (in-app purchase). Purchases are handled by Google Play. We receive a purchase record to unlock content; we never receive your payment card details.
  • Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. We collect app usage events (such as levels started and completed), device and app information, and crash reports to improve the App. This data is not used to identify you personally. You can turn analytics off in the App's settings.

Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Data security

Data sent to these services is encrypted in transit.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your choices and data deletion

You can reset all progress from the App's settings, opt out of analytics in settings, reset your advertising ID in your device settings, and delete Play Games data from your Google account. To request deletion of any data associated with you, email us at CONTACT_EMAIL.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
